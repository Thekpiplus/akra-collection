Amenities & services

Everything you need for everyday living — not a short check-in stay.

Estate living

Comfort with quiet support

AKRA Collection Layan pairs private residences with practical services so you can focus on life in Phuket — work, family, and downtime — while the estate team takes care of the rest.

Housekeeping & linen

Cleaning twice a week and linen changed once a week are included in your rental, so everyday living stays easy.

Pool & garden care

Every residence has a private saltwater pool and garden, professionally maintained as part of your lease.

24-hour reception

Round-the-clock reception and estate maintenance support while you live on site.

Vanali Café

An on-site café serving coffee, bakery items, and Thai and fusion dishes — open for residents to enjoy at their own pace.

High-speed Wi-Fi

Reliable connectivity suited to remote work and everyday streaming across the estate.

Vehicle & transfers

Front-desk assistance with vehicle rentals and optional airport transfers for a smoother move-in.

Beach shuttle

A complimentary afternoon shuttle to Layan Beach for easy coastal access without needing a car.

In-residence wellness

Massage therapists can be arranged to your villa through reception — privacy comes first.

Clear utility billing

Water and electricity are billed separately based on actual usage — transparent for long-term residents.
